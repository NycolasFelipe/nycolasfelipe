## Hey, I'm Nycolas Felipe! 👋

Software Engineer and cofounder of [Techlithy](https://techlithy.com/), where I lead the development of a multi-tenant SaaS CRM used by 4 companies that together run more than 20 schools. I work from product and architecture to running it in production.

I started in front-end at Opikode, moved to fullstack at Transforma Educação, where I was the tech lead of the Ensina Mais e-commerce, and since July 2026 I've been full time at Techlithy, which now also runs Transforma's IT.

🌐 **Portfolio and case studies:** [nycolasfelipe.techlithy.com](https://nycolasfelipe.techlithy.com/en)

### 🚀 Featured work

- **[Techlithy CRM](https://nycolasfelipe.techlithy.com/en/projects/crm-techlithy)**: multi-tenant SaaS for sales teams that sell through WhatsApp and Instagram, with a kanban pipeline, automated campaigns and AI agents. NestJS, React 19, PostgreSQL, Redis, BullMQ.
- **[Ensina Mais e-commerce](https://nycolasfelipe.techlithy.com/en/projects/ensina-mais)**: online store, point of sale at the schools and back-office for a publishing house, integrated with payments, ERP and invoicing. Node.js and TypeScript microservices, MySQL, RabbitMQ.
- **[Portal Transforma ERP](https://nycolasfelipe.techlithy.com/en/projects/portal-transforma)**: internal portal that brings enrollment, sales and finance reports together, with access by department and by unit. React 19, TanStack, Express 5, Redis.
